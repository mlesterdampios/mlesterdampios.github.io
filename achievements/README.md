## 🌐 About Us

**Shinigami.ph** is a Philippine cybersecurity team competing across CTFs and red-team events. We specialize in:
- Reverse engineering & pwn
- Web exploitation & appsec
- DFIR, threat hunting, and log forensics
- Crypto, misc, and OSINT

We value clean writeups, reproducible tooling, and mentoring newcomers.
